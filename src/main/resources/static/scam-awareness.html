What to do if you were scammed

1
Stop communication

Cut off contact with the suspected scammer immediately across phone, text, and email.

2
Protect your accounts

Change passwords and contact your bank or credit card provider right away.

3
Preserve evidence

Take screenshots of messages, phone numbers, and transaction details before deleting them.

4
Report the incident

File a report with local authorities and alert your service providers.
